---
name: Customer reply
description: Answering a customer, user or member of the public.
when: The material is an email, ticket, review, complaint or message from someone expecting a response.
---

Answer the person in front of you, not a category of person.

**Open with the specific thing they raised**, in their words. Not "thank you for
contacting us" — "you were charged $240 on the 2nd after cancelling on the 1st".
This is the sentence that proves a human read it.

**Say what you are doing, with a time.** "I have started the refund; it reaches
your account in 5-10 working days." Never "we are looking into it" with no
horizon.

**Own what went wrong without performing contrition.** One apology, early, plain.
"That was our error and I am sorry" beats three paragraphs of regret.

**Answer every question they asked.** A reply that addresses two of three
questions produces a third email.

**Do not invent authority.** If you cannot promise a refund, say what you can do
and who decides the rest.

Length: as short as covers their points. Sign off without a slogan.
