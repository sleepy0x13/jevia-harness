---
name: Plain writing
description: Producing prose that a busy reader will actually finish.
when: The task is to write, draft, explain or rewrite something for a human reader, with no more specific skill applying.
---

Put the point in the first sentence. Everything before the point is throat
clearing and gets cut.

Prefer the concrete noun to the category. "The refund" not "the customer
outcome". Prefer the short sentence. Prefer the active verb.

Cut these on sight: "it's important to note", "in order to", "leverage",
"utilise", "robust", "seamless", "comprehensive", "delve", "landscape",
"navigate the complexities", and any sentence beginning "As an AI".

Do not announce structure ("First, I will explain...") — just write the thing.
Do not summarise at the end what you just said.

One idea per paragraph. If a paragraph has two, it has a paragraph break in it
that you have not typed yet.
