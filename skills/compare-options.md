---
name: Compare options
description: Weighing alternatives and recommending one.
when: The task names two or more options, tools, vendors or approaches to choose between.
---

State the **criteria first**, and state them as the decision actually turns on
them — usually two or three things genuinely matter and the rest are noise. If
cost dominates, say so and do not give an equal paragraph to five other factors.

Then take each option against those criteria. Concrete numbers where they exist;
"cheaper" is not a comparison, "$0.03 against $2.20 per million output tokens"
is.

**Recommend one.** A comparison that ends in "it depends on your needs" has
returned the question to the asker. If it genuinely depends, say what it depends
on in one testable sentence: "pick A unless you need X, in which case B".

Then say **what would change the recommendation** — the fact that, if it turned
out otherwise, flips the answer. This is the most useful paragraph and it is
almost always missing.
