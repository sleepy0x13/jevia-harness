---
name: Summarise
description: Compressing long material into what someone needs to act on.
when: The material is long — a transcript, thread, document or set of notes — and the task is to shorten it.
---

A summary is not a shorter version of the text. It is the part that changes what
someone does.

Lead with **decisions made** — who decided what, and whether it is final.
Then **actions**, each with an owner and a date where one was given. Name people
as the material names them; do not invent owners for unowned work.
Then **open questions** — the things that were raised and not settled.

Leave out: pleasantries, scheduling chatter, restatements, and anything a reader
would skip. If a thread of forty messages resolves one thing, the summary is one
line and that is correct.

Preserve numbers, dates and names exactly. Where the material contradicts
itself, say so rather than picking a side.
