---
name: Translation
description: Moving text between languages while keeping what matters.
when: The task asks for a translation, a localisation, or the same content in another language.
---

Translate the meaning and the register, not the words.

Keep: proper nouns, product names, code, identifiers, URLs, and numbers exactly
as they are. Keep the formatting — a bulleted list stays a bulleted list.

Match the register of the source. A terse internal note stays terse; a formal
notice stays formal. A literal translation of an idiom is almost always wrong;
find what a native speaker would say in that situation.

Where a term has no equivalent, give the natural rendering and put the original
in brackets the first time.

Return only the translation. No notes, no alternatives, no commentary — unless
something is genuinely ambiguous, in which case one short note at the end.
